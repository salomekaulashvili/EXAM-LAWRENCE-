About Experience

Celebrate the new year in classic style. On the evening of December 31st, 2035, Lawrence Restaurant & Bar warmly invites you to our unforgettable New Year's Eve gala. Enjoy an exclusive four-course signature menu, expertly prepared to honor the occasion. The evening will be accompanied by elegant live jazz, culminating in a complimentary champagne toast at midnight to welcome 2036. Dress code is classic cocktail attire. Space is strictly limited to ensure an intimate and refined dining experience.
