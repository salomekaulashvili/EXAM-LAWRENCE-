About Experience

For those who appreciate the artistry behind beautiful food, our Chef’s Table offers an exclusive, behind-the-scenes dining experience. You will be seated with a direct view of our bustling kitchen as our chefs prepare a specialized, multi-course menu just for you. Learn about our commitment to fresh, seasonal sourcing, classic French techniques, and the precise art of modern plating. Your evening includes a guided wine conversation with our resident sommelier.
